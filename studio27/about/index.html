One studio. Many creatives.

Studio 27 is one studio built for many crafts — a place where different creatives can work, create, and grow. Creativity doesn't fit into one category, and neither do we.

The idea

One space for
every craft

The word "studio" already means a lot of things — a photo studio, a barber studio. Most of the time those all end up in different buildings, run by different people, with nothing connecting them.

Studio 27 puts them in the same room. A photographer and a barber, sharing one space instead of renting separate ones.

Different people, different crafts, the same address — and a little bit of each other's energy rubbing off along the way.
