About Legacy Hardscape ATX

Building Austin's Outdoor Legacy

We're an Austin-based hardscape and outdoor living contractor with a simple standard: build it the way we'd want it built on our own property.

Our Approach

Craftsmanship Over Shortcuts

Legacy Hardscape ATX exists because too much outdoor construction is built to survive one season, not one lifetime. A patio poured without proper base preparation looks identical to a well-built one — for about a year.

We build differently. Every driveway, patio, pergola, and outdoor kitchen we install starts with the unglamorous work most homeowners never see: excavation, grading, and base compaction done right, before a single decorative material goes down.

The name isn't incidental. A legacy is something built to outlast the moment it was made in — and that's the standard we hold every project to, whether it's a small paver walkway or a full backyard transformation.

We serve homeowners across Austin and the surrounding metro, handling the complete outdoor project — hardscape, structures, and landscape — as one accountable team, not a chain of subcontractors.
