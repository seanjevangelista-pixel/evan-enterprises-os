Common questions

Things people usually ask.

How fast do you respond?+

Within 24 hours, always — and it's always me replying, not a rep relaying things back and forth. Most of the time it's a lot faster than that.

I'm not sure which service I need — is that okay?+

Completely normal. That's what the first call is for — tell me what's going on with your business and I'll tell you honestly what would actually help, even if it's not the thing you asked about.

Can I cancel ad management if it's not working out?+

Yes — it's month-to-month with no contract lock-in and no early termination fee. Cancel anytime.

Do one-time projects come with any ongoing commitment?+

No. Websites and UGC work wrap up at final delivery — no retainer, no renewal, nothing automatic.

What if I want changes after the first draft?+

Revision rounds are part of the scope we agree on at kickoff, so this isn't a surprise bill — we'll talk through exactly how many rounds are included before anything starts.
