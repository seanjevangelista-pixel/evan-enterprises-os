Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Evan Enterprises LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates evanenterprise.com and provides marketing management, distribution product-research, and AI communication services for businesses. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly — such as your name, email address, phone number, business name, and any details you share through our contact forms, chat widget, phone calls, or when you become a client.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide our services
  • To send appointment confirmations, follow-ups, and service-related messages
  • To improve our services and communications

SMS / Text Messaging

By providing your phone number and opting in, you consent to receive SMS text messages from Evan Enterprises LLC (and businesses whose messaging we manage) related to your inquiry, appointments, and services.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for assistance.

We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or SMS opt-in consent with any third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. Mobile opt-in information and consent are never shared with third parties.

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us operate (for example, our messaging and CRM platform) under confidentiality obligations, or when required by law.

Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Your Choices

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by contacting us using the details below.
