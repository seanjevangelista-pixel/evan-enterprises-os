Client Onboarding

Working together — how this goes from here.

This covers how projects run across everything I do: ad management, website builds, and UGC. Some details shift per project — we'll confirm specifics on our kickoff call — but the structure below is consistent every time, so you know exactly what to expect before we start.

Step one

What we cover on the kickoff call.

Before anything starts, we get on a call to lock in scope — a real target, not a guess.

→
Ad Management
Monthly ad spend budget ($500/mo minimum) · ad account access · target service area · the goal we're optimizing toward (calls, form fills, bookings).
→
Website Building
Page count · who's responsible for copy and content · number of revision rounds included · target launch date · whether an AI chatbot add-on fits the build.
→
UGC Work
Number of pieces and formats · brand guidelines · approval process · usage rights (organic vs. paid ads) · turnaround time.
Step two

From kickoff to launch.

The exact timeline depends on the service, but the shape is the same every time.

01
Deposit + kickoff call
50% deposit to start, then a 15–20 minute call to confirm scope, budget, and timeline.
02
Build or setup
Website: design and development against the agreed scope. Ads: account setup, conversion tracking, campaign build. UGC: production against brief.
03
Review
You see the work before it goes live — a site walkthrough, an ad campaign screen-share, or content for approval, depending on the project.
04
Launch
Remaining balance due, then we go live. Ad management continues month-to-month with ongoing reporting; websites and UGC wrap at delivery.
